Posterior weighting

After marginalizing the scale, the inlier density is a Student-t; the selected partition's scale estimate and the per-point posterior inlier probabilities follow in closed form. That toolkit — the scale estimators (IGPrior, fit_scale, partition_student_t), the Student-t + uniform mixture (StudentTMixture, posterior_weights!, posterior_inlier_probs!), the posterior-weight state and its expectation–conditional-maximization (ECM) updates (PosteriorWeightState, update_scale!, update_nu!, update_inlier_prior!, mixture_ecm!) and their update policies (Estimated, FixedS2, FixedPrior, FixedNu, ConsensusDof) — is VisualGeometryCore's, documented there.

A scored partition seeds it through the scoring contract: after score! on a StudentTUniformScoring, the first inlier_count entries of consensus_order are the swept partition, and PosteriorWeightState(sq_residuals, n, model_dof, k; order, prior = scale_prior(scoring), …) is its Student-t with the E-step run once.
